        The whole document, one aggregate per page: matter (28), consciousness, feeling, perception and formations (the cetasikas, by their groups). Each reality is a small table: its characteristic, function, manifestation and proximate cause, the English beside the commentaries’ Pāli, with the references (DhsA, Vs.) under it.
