(1) The earth-element — pathavī-dhātu

EnglishPāli
Characteristic(It has the characteristic of) hardness.lakkhaṇā, kakkhaḷatta-lakkhaṇā,
Function(Its function is) to act as a foundation for the other coexisting primary elements and derived materialities in its own kalapa.rasā, patiṭṭhāna-rasā,
Manifestation(It is manifested) as receiving coexisting materialities in its own kalapa.paccupaṭṭhānā, sampaṭicchana-paccupa
Proximate cause(Its proximate course is) the other three great primary elements in its own kalapa. (water, fire and air)padaṭṭhānā. avasesa-dhātuttaya-pada
