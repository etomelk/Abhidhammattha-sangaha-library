(3 of Universals) The perception aggregate — Saññā-kkhando

Perception

saññā

(C) the perceiving (noting) of the qualities of the object.

sañjānana-lakkhaṇā.

(F) recognizing what has been previously perceived; or

paccābhiññāṇa-rasā, (DhsA.153)

to make a sign as a condition for perceiving again “this is

tadevetanti puna sañjānana-paccaya-

the same”,

nimitta-karaṇa-rasā,

as carpenters, etc., do in the case of timber, and so on.

dāru-ādīsu tacchakādayo viya.

(M) as the action of interpreting by way of the features that

yathā-gahita-nimittavasenaṭabhinivesa-

had been apprehended,

karaṇa-paccupaṭṭhānā,

like the blind who ‘see’ an elephant; or

hatthi-dassaka-andhā viya.

as briefness, owing to its inability to penetrate the object,

ārammaṇeṭanogāḷhavuttitāya aciraṭṭhāna-

like lighting.

paccupaṭṭhānā vā vijju viya. (DhsA.154)

(P) an object in whatever way that appears,

yathā-upaṭṭhita-visaya-padaṭṭhānā,

like the perception that arises in fawns (young deer)

tiṇa-purisakesu migapotakānaṃ

that see scarecrows as men.

‘purisā’tiṭuppanna-saññā viya. (Vs.2.92)
