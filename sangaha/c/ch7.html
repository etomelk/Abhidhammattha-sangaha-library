7. Samuccayaparicchedo

VII.1VibhāvinīNāradaDvāsattatividhā vuttā, vatthudhammā salakkhaṇā.

Tesaṃ dāni yathāyogaṃ, pavakkhāmi samuccayaṃ.

VII.2VibhāvinīAkusalasaṅgaho missakasaṅgaho bodhipakkhiyasaṅgaho sabbasaṅgaho ceti samuccayasaṅgaho catubbidho veditabbo.

Akusalasaṅgaho

VII.3VibhāvinīKathaṃ? Akusalasaṅgahe tāva cattāro āsavā – kāmāsavo bhavāsavo diṭṭhāsavo avijjāsavo.

VII.4VibhāvinīCattāro oghā – kāmogho bhavogho diṭṭhogho avijjogho.

VII.5VibhāvinīCattāro yogā – kāmayogo bhavayogo diṭṭhiyogo avijjāyogo.

VII.6VibhāvinīCattāro ganthā – abhijjhākāyagantho, byāpādo kāyagantho, sīlabbataparāmāso kāyagantho, idaṃsaccābhiniveso kāyagantho.

VII.7VibhāvinīCattāro 47 upādānā – kāmupādānaṃ diṭṭhupādānaṃ sīlabbatupādānaṃ attavādupādānaṃ.

VII.8VibhāvinīCha nīvaraṇāni – kāmacchandanīvaraṇaṃ byāpādanīvaraṇaṃ thinamiddhanīvaraṇaṃ uddhaccakukkuccanīvaraṇaṃ vicikicchānīvaraṇaṃ avijjānīvaraṇaṃ.

VII.9VibhāvinīSatta anusayā – kāmarāgānusayo bhavarāgānusayo paṭighānusayo mānānusayo diṭṭhānusayo vicikicchānusayo avijjānusayo.

VII.10VibhāvinīDasa saṃyojanāni – kāmarāgasaṃyojanaṃ rūparāgasaṃyojanaṃ arūparāgasaṃyojanaṃ paṭighasaṃyojanaṃ mānasaṃyojanaṃ diṭṭhisaṃyojanaṃ sīlabbataparāmāsasaṃyojanaṃ vicikicchāsaṃyojanaṃ uddhaccasaṃyojanaṃ avijjāsaṃyojanaṃ suttante.

VII.11Aparānipi dasa saṃyojanāni – kāmarāgasaṃyojanaṃ bhavarāgasaṃyojanaṃ paṭighasaṃyojanaṃ mānasaṃyojanaṃ diṭṭhisaṃyojanaṃ sīlabbataparāmāsasaṃyojanaṃ vicikicchāsaṃyojanaṃ issāsaṃyojanaṃ macchariyasaṃyojanaṃ avijjāsaṃyojanaṃ abhidhamme (vibha. 969).

VII.12VibhāvinīDasa kilesā – lobho doso moho māno diṭṭhi vicikicchā thinaṃ uddhaccaṃ ahirikaṃ anottappaṃ.

VII.13VibhāvinīĀsavādīsu panettha kāmabhavanāmena tabbatthukā taṇhā adhippetā, sīlabbataparāmāso idaṃsaccābhiniveso attavādupādo ca tathāpavattaṃ diṭṭhigatameva pavuccati.

VII.14VibhāvinīĀsavoghā ca yogā ca,

Tayo ganthā ca vatthuto;

Upādānā duve vuttā,

Aṭṭha nīvaraṇā siyuṃ.

Chaḷevānusayā 48 honti, nava saṃyojanā matā;

Kilesā dasa vuttoyaṃ, navadhā pāpasaṅgaho.

Missakasaṅgaho

VII.15VibhāvinīNāradaMissakasaṅgahe cha hetū – lobho doso moho alobho adoso amoho.

VII.16VibhāvinīSatta jhānaṅgāni – vitakko vicāro pīti ekaggatā somanassaṃ domanassaṃ upekkhā.

VII.17VibhāvinīDvādasa maggaṅgāni – sammādiṭṭhi sammāsaṅkappo sammāvācā sammākammanto sammāājīvo sammāvāyāmo sammāsati sammāsamādhi micchādiṭṭhi micchāsaṅkappo micchāvāyāmo micchāsamādhi.

VII.18VibhāvinīBāvīsatindriyāni – cakkhundriyaṃ sotindriyaṃ ghānindriyaṃ jivhindriyaṃ kāyindriyaṃ itthindriyaṃ purisindriyaṃ jīvitindriyaṃ manindriyaṃ sukhindriyaṃ dukkhindriyaṃ somanassindriyaṃ domanassindriyaṃ upekkhindriyaṃ saddhindriyaṃ vīriyindriyaṃ satindriyaṃ samādhindriyaṃ paññindriyaṃ anaññātaññassāmītindriyaṃ aññindriyaṃ aññātāvindriyaṃ.

VII.19VibhāvinīNava balāni – saddhābalaṃ vīriyabalaṃ satibalaṃ samādhibalaṃ paññābalaṃ hiribalaṃ ottappabalaṃ ahirikabalaṃ anottappabalaṃ.

VII.20VibhāvinīCattāro adhipatī – chandādhipati vīriyādhipati cittādhipati vīmaṃsādhipati.

VII.21VibhāvinīCattāro āhārā – kabaḷīkāro āhāro, phasso dutiyo, manosañcetanā tatiyā, viññāṇaṃ catutthaṃ.

VII.22Indriyesu 49 panettha sotāpattimaggañāṇaṃ anaññātaññassāmītindriyaṃ.

VII.23Arahattaphalañāṇaṃ aññātāvindriyaṃ.

VII.24Majjhe cha ñāṇāni aññindriyānīti pavuccanti.

VII.25Jīvitindriyañca rūpārūpavasena duvidhaṃ hoti.

VII.26VibhāvinīPañcaviññāṇesu jhānaṅgāni, avīriyesu balāni, ahetukesu maggaṅgāni na labbhanti.

VII.27VibhāvinīTathā vicikicchācitte ekaggatā maggindriyabalabhāvaṃ na gacchati.

VII.28VibhāvinīDvihetukatihetukajavanesveva yathāsambhavaṃ adhipati ekova labbhatīti.

VII.29VibhāvinīCha hetū pañca jhānaṅgā, maggaṅgā nava vatthuto.

Soḷasindriyadhammā ca, baladhammā naveritā.

Cattārodhipati vuttā, tathāhārāti sattadhā;

Kusalādisamākiṇṇo, vuttomissakasaṅgaho.

Bodhipakkhiyasaṅgaho

VII.30VibhāvinīBodhipakkhiyasaṅgahe cattāro satipaṭṭhānā kāyānupassanāsatipaṭṭhānaṃ vedanānupassanāsatipaṭṭhānaṃ cittānupassanāsatipaṭṭhānaṃ dhammānupassanāsatipaṭṭhānaṃ.

VII.31VibhāvinīCattāro sammappadhānā uppannānaṃ pāpakānaṃ pahānāya vāyāmo, anuppannānaṃ pāpakānaṃ anuppādāya vāyāmo, anuppannānaṃ kusalānaṃ uppādāya vāyāmo, uppannānaṃ kusalānaṃ bhiyyobhāvāya vāyāmo.

VII.32VibhāvinīCattāro 50 iddhipādā – chandiddhipādo vīriyiddhipādo cittiddhipādo vīmaṃsiddhipādo.

VII.33Pañcindriyāni – saddhindriyaṃ vīriyindriyaṃ satindriyaṃ samādhindriyaṃ paññindriyaṃ.

VII.34Pañca balāni – saddhābalaṃ vīriyabalaṃ satibalaṃ samādhibalaṃ paññābalaṃ.

VII.35VibhāvinīSatta bojjhaṅgā – satisambojjhaṅgo dhammavicayasambojjhaṅgo vīriyasambojjhaṅgo pītisambojjhaṅgo passaddhisambojjhaṅgo samādhisambojjhaṅgo upekkhāsambojjhaṅgo.

VII.36Aṭṭha maggaṅgāni – sammādiṭṭhi sammāsaṅkappo sammāvācā sammākammanto sammāājīvo sammāvāyāmo sammāsati sammāsamādhi.

VII.37Ettha pana cattāro satipaṭṭhānāti sammāsati ekāva pavuccati.

VII.38Tathā cattāro sammappadhānāti ca sammāvāyāmo.

VII.39Chando cittamupekkhā ca, saddhāpassaddhipītiyo.

Sammādiṭṭhi ca saṅkappo, vāyāmo viratittayaṃ.

Sammāsati samādhīti, cuddasete sabhāvato;

Sattatiṃsappabhedena, sattadhā tattha saṅgaho.

VII.40VibhāvinīSaṅkappapassaddhi ca pītupekkhā,

Chando ca cittaṃ viratittayañca;

Navekaṭhānā viriyaṃ navaṭṭha,

Satī samādhī catu pañca paññā;

Saddhā duṭhānuttamasattatiṃsa-

Dhammānameso pavaro vibhāgo.

VII.41VibhāvinīSabbe 51 lokuttare honti, na vā saṅkappapītiyo.

Lokiyepi yathāyogaṃ, chabbisuddhipavattiyaṃ.

Sabbasaṅgaho

VII.42VibhāvinīNāradaSabbasaṅgahe pañcakkhandhā – rūpakkhandho vedanākkhandho saññākkhandho saṅkhārakkhandho viññāṇakkhandho.

VII.43VibhāvinīPañcupādānakkhandhā – rūpupādānakkhandho vedanupādānakkhandho saññupādānakkhandho saṅkhārupādānakkhandho viññāṇupādānakkhandho.

VII.44VibhāvinīDvādasāyatanāni – cakkhāyatanaṃ sotāyatanaṃ ghānāyatanaṃ jivhāyatanaṃ kāyāyatanaṃ manāyatanaṃ rūpāyatanaṃ saddāyatanaṃ gandhāyatanaṃ rasāyatanaṃ phoṭṭhabbāyatanaṃ dhammāyatanaṃ.

VII.45VibhāvinīAṭṭhārasa dhātuyo – cakkhudhātu sotadhātu ghānadhātu jivhādhātu kāyadhātu rūpadhātu saddadhātu gandhadhātu rasadhātu phoṭṭhabbadhātu cakkhuviññāṇadhātu sotaviññāṇadhātu ghānaviññāṇadhātu jivhāviññāṇadhātu kāyaviññāṇadhātu manodhātu dhammadhātu manoviññāṇadhātu.

VII.46VibhāvinīCattāri ariyasaccāni – dukkhaṃ ariyasaccaṃ, dukkhasamudayo ariyasaccaṃ, dukkhanirodho ariyasaccaṃ, dukkhanirodhagāminī paṭipadā ariyasaccaṃ.

VII.47VibhāvinīEttha pana cetasikasukhumarūpanibbānavasena ekūnasattati dhammā dhammāyatanadhammadhātūti saṅkhaṃ gacchanti.

VII.48Manāyatanameva sattaviññāṇadhātuvasena bhijjati.

VII.49VibhāvinīRūpañca vedanā saññā, sesacetasikā tathā.

Viññāṇamiti pañcete, pañcakkhandhāti bhāsitā.

VII.50VibhāvinīPañcupādānakkhandhāti52, tathā tebhūmakā matā.

Bhedābhāvena nibbānaṃ, khandhasaṅgahanissaṭaṃ.

VII.51VibhāvinīDvārārammaṇabhedena, bhavantāyatanāni ca.

Dvārālambataduppanna-pariyāyena dhātuyo.

VII.52VibhāvinīDukkhaṃ tebhūmakaṃ vaṭṭaṃ, taṇhā samudayo bhave.

Nirodho nāma nibbānaṃ, maggo lokuttaro mato.

VII.53VibhāvinīMaggayuttā phalā ceva, catusaccavinissaṭā.

Iti pañcappabhedena, pavutto sabbasaṅgaho.

Iti abhidhammatthasaṅgahe samuccayasaṅgahavibhāgo nāma

Sattamo paricchedo.
