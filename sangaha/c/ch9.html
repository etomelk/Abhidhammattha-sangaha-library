9. Kammaṭṭhānaparicchedo

IX.1VibhāvinīSamathavipassanānaṃ, bhāvanānamito paraṃ.

Kammaṭṭhānaṃ pavakkhāmi, duvidhampi yathākkamaṃ.

Samathakammaṭṭhānaṃ

IX.2NāradaTattha samathasaṅgahe tāva dasa kasiṇāni, dasa asubhā, dasa anussatiyo, catasso appamaññāyo, ekā saññā, ekaṃ vavatthānaṃ, cattāro āruppā ceti sattavidhena samathakammaṭṭhānasaṅgaho.

Caritabhedo

IX.3VibhāvinīRāgacaritā 59 dosacaritā mohacaritā saddhācaritā buddhicaritā vitakkacaritā ceti chabbidhena caritasaṅgaho.

Bhāvanābhedo

IX.4VibhāvinīParikammabhāvanā upacārabhāvanā appanābhāvanā ceti tisso bhāvanā.

Nimittabhedo

IX.5VibhāvinīParikammanimittaṃ uggahanimittaṃ paṭibhāganimittañceti tīṇi nimittāni ca veditabbāni.

IX.6VibhāvinīKathaṃ? Pathavīkasiṇaṃ āpokasiṇaṃ tejokasiṇaṃ vāyokasiṇaṃ nīlakasiṇaṃ pītakasiṇaṃ lohitakasiṇaṃ odātakasiṇaṃ ākāsakasiṇaṃ ālokakasiṇañceti imāni dasa kasiṇāni nāma.

IX.7VibhāvinīUddhumātakaṃ vinīlakaṃ vipubbakaṃ vicchiddakaṃ vikkhāyitakaṃ vikkhittakaṃ hatavikkhittakaṃ lohitakaṃ puḷavakaṃ aṭṭhikañceti ime dasa asubhā nāma.

IX.8VibhāvinīBuddhānussati dhammānussati saṃghānussati sīlānussati cāgānussati devatānussati upasamānussati maraṇānussati kāyagatāsati ānāpānassati ceti imā dasa anussatiyo nāma.

IX.9VibhāvinīMettā karuṇā muditā upekkhā ceti imā catasso appamaññāyo nāma, brahmavihāroti ca pavuccati.

IX.10VibhāvinīĀhārepaṭikūlasaññā ekā saññā nāma.

IX.11VibhāvinīCatudhātuvavatthānaṃ ekaṃ vavatthānaṃ nāma.

IX.12VibhāvinīĀkāsānañcāyatanādayo 60 cattāro āruppā nāmāti sabbathāpi samathaniddese cattālīsa kammaṭṭhānāni bhavanti.

Sappāyabhedo

IX.13VibhāvinīNāradaCaritāsu pana dasa asubhā kāyagatāsatisaṅkhātā koṭṭhāsabhāvanā ca rāgacaritassa sappāyā.

IX.14Catasso appamaññāyo nīlādīni ca cattāri kasiṇāni dosacaritassa.

IX.15Ānāpānaṃ mohacaritassa vitakkacaritassa ca,

IX.16Buddhānussatiādayo cha saddhācaritassa.

IX.17VibhāvinīMaraṇaupasamasaññāvavatthānāni buddhicaritassa.

IX.18VibhāvinīSesāni pana sabbānipi kammaṭṭhānāni sabbesampi sappāyāni, tatthāpi kasiṇesu puthulaṃ mohacaritassa, khuddakaṃ vitakkacaritassevāti.

Ayamettha sappāyabhedo.

Bhāvanābhedo

IX.19VibhāvinīNāradaBhāvanāsu sabbatthāpi parikammabhāvanā labbhateva, buddhānussatiādīsu aṭṭhasu saññāvavatthānesu cāti dasasukammaṭṭhānesu upacārabhāvanāva sampajjati, natthi appanā.

IX.20Sesesu pana samatiṃsakammaṭṭhānesu appanābhāvanāpi sampajjati.

IX.21VibhāvinīTatthāpi dasa kasiṇāni ānāpānañca pañcakajjhānikāni.

IX.22VibhāvinīDasa 61 asubhā kāyagatāsati ca paṭhamajjhānikā.

IX.23VibhāvinīMettādayo tayo catukkajjhānikā.

IX.24VibhāvinīUpekkhā pañcamajjhānikāti chabbīsati rūpāvacarajjhānikāni kammaṭṭhānāni.

IX.25Cattāro pana āruppā āruppajjhānikāti.

Ayamettha bhāvanābhedo.

Gocarabhedo

IX.26VibhāvinīNāradaNimittesu pana parikammanimittaṃ uggahanimittañca sabbatthāpi yathārahaṃ pariyāyena labbhanteva.

IX.27VibhāvinīPaṭibhāganimittaṃ pana kasiṇāsubhakoṭṭhāsaānāpānesveva labbhati, tattha hi paṭibhāganimittamārabbha upacārasamādhi appanāsamādhi ca pavattanti.

IX.28VibhāvinīKathaṃ? Ādikammikassa hi pathavīmaṇḍalādīsu nimittaṃ uggaṇhantassa tamārammaṇaṃ parikammanimittanti pavuccati, sā ca bhāvanā parikammabhāvanā nāma.

IX.29VibhāvinīYadā pana taṃ nimittaṃ cittena samuggahitaṃ hoti, cakkhunā passantasseva manodvārassa āpāthamāgataṃ, tadā tamevārammaṇaṃ uggahanimittaṃ nāma, sā ca bhāvanā samādhiyati.

IX.30VibhāvinīTathā samāhitassa panetassa tato paraṃ tasmiṃ uggahanimitte parikammasamādhinā bhāvanamanuyuñjantassa yadā tappaṭibhāgaṃ vatthudhammavimuccitaṃ paññattisaṅkhātaṃ bhāvanāmayamārammaṇaṃ citte sannisannaṃ samappitaṃ hoti, tadā taṃ paṭibhāganimittaṃ samuppannanti pavuccati.

IX.31VibhāvinīTato paṭṭhāya paripanthavippahīnā kāmāvacarasamādhisaṅkhātā upacārabhāvanā nipphannā nāma hoti.

IX.32Tato 62 paraṃ tameva paribhāganimittaṃ upacārasamādhinā samāsevantassa rūpāvacarapaṭhamajjhānamappeti.

IX.33VibhāvinīTato paraṃ tameva paṭhamajjhānaṃ āvajjanaṃ samāpajjanaṃ adhiṭṭhānaṃ vuṭṭhānaṃ paccavekkhaṇā ceti imāhi pañcahi vasitāhi vasībhūtaṃ katvā vitakkādikamoḷārikaṅgaṃ pahānāya vicārādisukhumaṅgupattiyā padahato yathākkamaṃ dutiyajjhānādayo yathārahamappenti.

IX.34Iccevaṃ pathavīkasiṇādīsu dvāvīsatikammaṭṭhānesu paṭibhāganimittamupalabbhati.

IX.35Avasesesu pana appamaññā sattapaññattiyaṃ pavattanti.

IX.36VibhāvinīĀkāsavajjitakasiṇesu pana yaṃ kiñci kasiṇaṃ ugghāṭetvā laddhamākāsaṃ anantavasena parikammaṃ karontassa paṭhamāruppamappeti.

IX.37Tameva paṭhamāruppaviññāṇaṃ anantavasena parikammaṃ karontassa dutiyāruppamappeti.

IX.38Tameva paṭhamāruppaviññāṇābhāvaṃ pana ‘‘natthi kiñcī’’ti parikammaṃ karontassa tatiyāruppamappeti.

IX.39VibhāvinīTatiyāruppaṃ ‘‘santametaṃ, paṇītameta’’nti parikammaṃ karontassa catutthāruppamappeti.

IX.40VibhāvinīAvasesesu ca dasasu kammaṭṭhānesu buddhaguṇādikamārammaṇamārabbha parikammaṃ katvā tasmiṃ nimitte sādhukamuggahite tattheva parikammañca samādhiyati, upacāro ca sampajjati.

IX.41VibhāvinīAbhiññāvasena 63 pavattamānaṃ pana rūpāvacarapañcamajjhānaṃ abhiññāpādakapañcamajjhānā vuṭṭhahitvā adhiṭṭheyyādikamāvajjetvā parikammaṃ karontassa rūpādīsu ārammaṇesu yathārahamappeti.

IX.42VibhāvinīAbhiññā ca nāma –

Iddhividhaṃ dibbasotaṃ, paracittavijānanā;

Pubbenivāsānussati, dibbacakkhūti pañcadhā.

Ayamettha gocarabhedo.

Niṭṭhito ca samathakammaṭṭhānanayo.

Vipassanākammaṭṭhānaṃ

Visuddhibhedo

IX.43VibhāvinīNāradaVipassanākammaṭṭhāne pana sīlavisuddhi cittavisuddhi diṭṭhivisuddhi kaṅkhāvitaraṇavisuddhi maggāmaggañāṇadassanavisuddhi paṭipadāñāṇadassanavisuddhi ñāṇadassanavisuddhi ceti sattavidhena visuddhisaṅgaho.

IX.44VibhāvinīAniccalakkhaṇaṃ dukkhalakkhaṇaṃ anattalakkhaṇañceti tīṇi lakkhaṇāni.

IX.45VibhāvinīAniccānupassanā dukkhānupassanā anattānupassanā ceti tisso anupassanā.

IX.46VibhāvinīSammasanañāṇaṃ udayabbayañāṇaṃ bhaṅgañāṇaṃ bhayañāṇaṃ ādīnavañāṇaṃ nibbidāñāṇaṃ muccitukamyatāñāṇaṃ paṭisaṅkhāñāṇaṃ saṅkhārupekkhāñāṇaṃ anulomañāṇañceti dasa vipassanāñāṇāni.

IX.47VibhāvinīSuññato vimokkho, animitto vimokkho, appaṇihito vimokkho ceti tayo vimokkhā.

IX.48Suññatānupassanā 64 animittānupassanā appaṇihitānupassānā ceti tīṇi vimokkhamukhāni ca veditabbāni.

IX.49VibhāvinīKathaṃ? Pātimokkhasaṃvarasīlaṃ indriyasaṃvarasīlaṃ ājīvapārisuddhisīlaṃ paccayasannissitasīlañceti catupārisuddhisīlaṃ sīlavisuddhi nāma.

IX.50VibhāvinīUpacārasamādhi appanāsamādhi ceti duvidhopi samādhi cittavisuddhi nāma.

IX.51VibhāvinīLakkhaṇarasapaccupaṭṭhānapadaṭṭhānavasena nāmarūpa pariggaho diṭṭhivisuddhi nāma.

IX.52VibhāvinīTesameva ca nāmarūpānaṃ paccayapariggaho kaṅkhāvitaraṇavisuddhi nāma.

IX.53VibhāvinīTato paraṃ pana tathāpariggahitesu sappaccayesu tebhūmakasaṅkhāresu atītādibhedabhinnesu khandhādinayamārabbha kalāpavasena saṅkhipitvā ‘‘aniccaṃ khayaṭṭhena, dukkhaṃ bhayaṭṭhena, anattā asārakaṭṭhenā’’ti addhānavasena santativasena khaṇavasena vā sammasanañāṇena lakkhaṇattayaṃ sammasantassa tesveva paccayavasena khaṇavasena ca udayabbayañāṇena udayabbayaṃ samanupassantassa ca –

‘‘Obhāso pīti passaddhi, adhimokkho ca paggaho;

Sukhaṃ ñāṇamupaṭṭhānamupekkhā ca nikanti ce’’ti. –

Obhāsādivipassanupakkilesaparipanthapariggahavasena maggāmaggalakkhaṇavavatthānaṃ maggāmaggañāṇadassanavisuddhi nāma.

IX.54VibhāvinīTathā paripanthavimuttassa pana tassa udayabbayañāṇato paṭṭhāya yā vānulomā tilakkhaṇaṃ vipassanāparamparāya paṭipajjantassa nava vipassanāñāṇāni paṭipadāñāṇadassanavisuddhi nāma.

IX.55VibhāvinīTassevaṃ paṭipajjantassa 65 pana vipassanāparipākamāgamma ‘‘idāni appanā uppajjissatī’’ti bhavaṅgaṃ vocchijjitvā uppannamanodvārāvajjanānantaraṃ dve tīṇi vipassanācittāni yaṃ kiñci aniccādilakkhaṇamārabbha parikammopacārānulomanāmena pavattanti.

IX.56VibhāvinīYā sikhāppattā, sā sānulomā saṅkhārupekkhā vuṭṭhānagāminivipassanāti ca pavuccati.

IX.57VibhāvinīTato paraṃ gotrabhucittaṃ nibbānamālambitvā puthujjanagottamabhibhavantaṃ, ariyagottamabhisambhontañca pavattati.

IX.58VibhāvinīTassānantarameva maggo dukkhasaccaṃ parijānanto samudayasaccaṃ pajahanto, nirodhasaccaṃ sacchikaronto, maggasaccaṃ bhāvanāvasena appanāvīthimotarati.

IX.59VibhāvinīTato paraṃ dve tīṇi phalacittāni pavattitvā bhavaṅgapātova hoti, puna bhavaṅgaṃ vocchinditvā paccavekkhaṇañāṇāni pavattanti.

IX.60VibhāvinīMaggaṃ phalañca nibbānaṃ, paccavekkhati paṇḍito.

Hīne kilese sese ca, paccavekkhati vāna vā.

Chabbisuddhikamenevaṃ, bhāvetabbo catubbidho;

Ñāṇadassanavisuddhi, nāma maggo pavuccati.

Ayamettha visuddhibhedo.

Vimokkhabhedo

IX.61VibhāvinīNāradaTattha anattānupassanā attābhinivesaṃ muñcantī suññatānupassanā nāma vimokkhamukhaṃ hoti.

IX.62VibhāvinīAniccānupassanā 66 vipallāsanimittaṃ muñcantī animittānupassanā nāma.

IX.63VibhāvinīDukkhānupassanā taṇhāpaṇidhiṃ muñcantī appaṇihitānupassanā nāma.

IX.64VibhāvinīTasmā yadi vuṭṭhānagāminivipassanā anattato vipassati, suññato vimokkho nāma hoti maggo.

IX.65Yadi aniccato vipassati, animitto vimokkho nāma.

IX.66VibhāvinīYadi dukkhato vipassati, appaṇihito vimokkho nāmāti ca maggo vipassanāgamanavasena tīṇi nāmāni labhati, tathā phalañca maggāgamanavasena maggavīthiyaṃ.

IX.67VibhāvinīPhalasamāpattivīthiyaṃ pana yathāvuttanayena vipassantānaṃ yathāsakaphalamuppajjamānampi vipassanāgamanavaseneva suññatādivimokkhoti ca pavuccati, ārammaṇavasena pana sarasavasena ca nāmattayaṃ sabbattha sabbesampi samameva ca.

Ayamettha vimokkhabhedo.

Puggalabhedo

IX.68VibhāvinīNāradaEttha pana sotāpattimaggaṃ bhāvetvā diṭṭhivicikicchāpahānena pahīnāpāyagamano sattakkhattuparamo sotāpanno nāma hoti.

IX.69VibhāvinīSakadāgāmimaggaṃ bhāvetvā rāgadosamohānaṃ tanukarattā sakadāgāmī nāma hoti sakideva imaṃ lokaṃ āgantvā.

IX.70VibhāvinīAnāgāmimaggaṃ 67 bhāvetvā kāmarāgabyāpādānamanavasesappahānena anāgāmī nāma hoti anāgantvā itthattaṃ.

IX.71Arahattamaggaṃ bhāvetvā anavasesakilesappahānena arahā nāma hoti khīṇāsavo loke aggadakkhiṇeyyoti.

Ayamettha puggalabhedo.

Samāpattibhedo

IX.72VibhāvinīNāradaPhalasamāpattivīthiyaṃ panettha sabbesampi yathāsakaphalavasena sādhāraṇāva.

IX.73VibhāvinīNirodhasamāpattisamāpajjanaṃ pana anāgāmīnañceva arahantānañca labbhati, tattha yathākkamaṃ paṭhamajjhānādimahaggatasamāpattiṃ samāpajjitvā vuṭṭhāya tattha gate saṅkhāradhamme tattha tattheva vipassanto yāva ākiñcaññāyatanaṃ gantvā tato paraṃ adhiṭṭheyyādikaṃ pubbakiccaṃ katvā nevasaññānāsaññāyatanaṃ samāpajjati, tassa dvinnaṃ appanājavanānaṃ parato vocchijjati cittasantati, tato nirodhasamāpanno nāma hoti.

IX.74Vuṭṭhānakāle pana anāgāmino anāgāmiphalacittaṃ, arahato arahattaphalacittaṃ ekavārameva pavattitvā bhavaṅgapāto hoti, tato paraṃ paccavekkhaṇañāṇaṃ pavattati.

Ayamettha samāpattibhedo.

Niṭṭhito ca vipassanākammaṭṭhānanayo.

Uyyojanaṃ

IX.75VibhāvinīBhāvetabbaṃ 68 paniccevaṃ, bhāvanādvayamuttamaṃ.

Paṭipattirasassādaṃ, patthayantena sāsaneti.

Iti abhidhammatthasaṅgahe kammaṭṭhānasaṅgahavibhāgo nāma

Navamo paricchedo.
