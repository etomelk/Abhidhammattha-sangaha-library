Ācariya Anuruddha · the Sixth Council (Chaṭṭha Saṅgāyana) text, Yangon · digital edition of the Vipassana Research Institute

The whole text, one chapter per page: the opening verses, the nine paricchedas and the closing verses. Each paragraph carries the Saṅgaha’s own number (I.1, I.2 … IX.75), the map that every book of this library follows; a small chip beside it opens the same paragraph in the Vibhāvinī. The pages of the Myanmar edition are marked in the margin.
