        The whole course, one lesson per page, with its diagrams (each kept as an image, with its caption) and its footnotes set under the paragraphs that cite them. The lessons on consciousness, mental factors, matter, realms and processes are linked on the Saṅgaha map to the Saṅgaha’s chapters I, II, VI, V and IV.
