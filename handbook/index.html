    Volumes I–II: the Introduction and Chapters One to Six.
    Abhidhammattha-saṅgaha Library SBVMS, Selangor, 2012 (Vols. I–II)

    Handbook of Abhidhamma Studies

    Venerable Sayādaw U Sīlānanda · Selangor Buddhist Vipassana Meditation Society (SBVMS), Malaysia, 2012

    Volumes I and II, one chapter per page: Volume I has the Introduction and Chapters One to Three (of Three only feeling, roots and functions); Volume II has Chapters Four to Six. Each chapter is the Saṅgaha’s chapter of the same number, and the Saṅgaha map links it to the other books. The lectures follow Bhikkhu Bodhi’s Comprehensive Manual (CMA), whose sections they cite. A third volume (Chapters Seven to Nine) is not in this library.
