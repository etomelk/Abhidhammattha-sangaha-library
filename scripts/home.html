About this library

Reading

  • One part of a book per page, with its contents beside it.
  • Each book keeps its own numbering. Enter a number in the box at the top to go straight to it.
  • Every numbered section and printed page can be linked to.
  • Search finds words within a book; accents are optional, so rupa finds rūpa.
  • A book’s own index or glossary, where it has one, opens beside the text.

Between the books

  • The mātikā map links each triplet and couplet to every book that treats it.
  • The Pāli terms list gathers the indexed words of all the books.
  • Sections link across books: the Sixth Council numbers (Khine, the Pāli, the commentaries, the study notes) and the PTS numbers (Müller, Rhys Davids).
  • The Expositor links page by page to the Aṭṭhasālinī it translates, and back.

The texts

  • Each book follows its printed edition: the author’s wording, spelling and numbering are kept.
  • In the published books, a correction checked against the Pāli is shown with a dotted underline; point at it, or tap it, to see what was printed and the source.
  • The two sets of study notes were drafts, and their corrections are made in place.
  • The Expositor was transcribed from a scan and read twice against it: its own misprints are kept and marked [sic], and the few unreadable places [?].
  • Your reading position and settings are stored only in this browser.
