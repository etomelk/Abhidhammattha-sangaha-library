About this library

Reading

  • One part of a book per page, with its contents beside it.
  • Each book keeps its own numbering. Enter a number in the box at the top to go straight to it.
  • The Saṅgaha’s paragraphs are numbered by chapter: type 4.6 for IV.6.
  • Every numbered section and printed page can be linked to.
  • Search finds words within a book; accents are optional, so rupa finds rūpa.
  • A book’s own index or glossary, where it has one, opens beside the text.

Between the books

  • The Saṅgaha map lists every paragraph of the Saṅgaha with the same place in each book.
  • The Vibhāvinī is numbered by the Saṅgaha paragraph it explains; a chip beside each paragraph opens the other.
  • The Anudīpanī is numbered by the Paramatthadīpanī paragraph it explains, and linked to it the same way.

The texts

  • The Pāli books are the Sixth Council text as the Vipassana Research Institute publishes it, unchanged; their paragraph and page numbers are the edition’s.
  • Where the edition prints a paragraph number twice or out of order, it is kept in the text and not made a link.
  • Your reading position and settings are stored only in this browser.
