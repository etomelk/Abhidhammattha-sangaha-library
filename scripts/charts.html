To see at a glance

Charts

The tables and diagrams of the Abhidhamma that students in Myanmar draw and learn by heart — the 89 and 121 types of consciousness, the 52 mental factors and their combinations, the miscellaneous section, the cognitive process, the realms and matter — in the order of the Saṅgaha’s chapters. Open a chart to see it full-screen: it stays sharp at any zoom.

The charts are drawn by Aggañāṇī (© 2005, with the Centre for Buddhist Studies, Sagaing, for the course of Fundamental Abhidhamma; updated 2008) and by Dr. Rewata Dhamma (the realms and the eye-door process). Each is shown from its own PDF as a vector drawing, cropped to its printed area and unchanged; its words are given under it as text, and the PDF can be downloaded. In the viewer: scroll or pinch to zoom, drag to move, ← → for the next page or chart, ⟳ (or r) to turn a wide chart, Esc to close. On a phone held upright, wide charts are turned to fill the screen.
