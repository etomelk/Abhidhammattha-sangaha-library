Following this, any one of the twenty-nine sense-sphere javana cittas runs for seven mind moments. After the javanas, two registration resultants arise accordingly. Thus, seventeen mind moments have been completed. This cognitive process is called “Tadārammaṇa vāra atimahantārammana cakkhudvāra vīthi”, meaning the eye-door cognitive process with very great intensity object and ending with registration.
