### *Paṭisandhi* after *Cuti*

In *saṃsāra* all beings, except an *arahant,* will be born in a new life immediately after death. All kinds of rebirth (*paṭisandhi*) are preceded by death (*cuti*). Therefore there must be a classification of the types of *paṭisandhi* that are possible after the respective types of *cuti*.
