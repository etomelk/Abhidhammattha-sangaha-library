The *puthujjana*s are fourfold:

1. *Duggati Ahetuka* = a being whose *paṭisandhi* is without *hetu* and he is in a woeful plane.
2. *Sugati* Ahetuka = a being whose *paṭisandhi* is without *hetu*, but he is in a blissful plane.
3. *Dvihetuka* = a being whose *paṭisandhi* is with 2 *hetus* (in a blissful plane).
4. *Tihetuka* = a being whose *paṭisandhi* is with 3 *hetus* (in a blissful plane).

**Ariya**: *Ariya* means those who are far from mental defilements. In another way, *ariya*s are noble persons through their noble mind.

*Ariya*s are eightfold:

1. *Maggaṭṭha* = those whose stages are the paths, are 4.

2. *Phalaṭṭha* = those whose stages are fruitions, are 4.

### *Vīthicitta*s and Individuals

Regarding individuals the 80 *vīthicitta*s are divided accordingly. They run as follows:

(1) **Duggati Ahetuka**: Within such a being, 37 *vīthicitta*s are possible. They are:

```
Akusala                              12
Ahetuka (except hasituppāda)         17
Mahākusala                            8
```

(2) **Sugati Ahetuka**: Within such a being, 41 *vīthicitta*s are possible. They are:

```
Akusala                              12
Ahetuka (except hasituppāda)         17
Mahākusala                            8
Mahāvipāka without knowledge          4
```
