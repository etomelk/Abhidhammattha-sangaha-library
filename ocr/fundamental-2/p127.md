In the *kāmāvacara* planes, all 80 *vīthicitta*s are possible.

In the *rūpāvacara* planes, 64 *vīthicitta*s are possible while 16 *vīthicitta*s - 2 *dosamūla*, 6 *viññāāṇa citta*s (nose, tongue, body) and 8 *mahāvipāka citta*s - are impossible.

In *arūpāvacara* planes, 42 *vīthicitta*s are possible. They are:

| | |
|---|:-:|
| *Lobhamūla* | 8 |
| *Mohamūla* | 2 |
| *Manodvārāvajjana* | 1 |
| *Mahākusala* | 8 |
| *Mahākiriya* | 8 |
| *Arūpa kusala* and *kriya* | 8 |
| *Lokuttara* except *Sotāpattimagga* | 7 |
| Total *citta* | 42 |

In *asaññasatta* plane no *citta*s arise. Therefore, in that plane every kind of *vīthicitta*s is impossible.

**(B) Individual:** There are 12 types of individuals. They are:

| | |
|---|:-:|
| 1. *Puthujjana* | 4 |
| 2. *Ariya* | 8 |
| Total | 12 |

**Puthujjana:** '*Puthu*' means majority. '*Jana*' means beings. So majority beings are called *puthujjana*.
