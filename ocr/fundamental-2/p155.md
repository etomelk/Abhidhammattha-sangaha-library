**Special Notes:**

**In death process** in 6 doors, the *bhavaṅga* and *cuti* (death consciousness) are one of those 10 *kāma bhavaṅga citta*s that run with one of these three objects: *kamma*, *kammanimitta* and *gatinimitta*. *Javana citta*s must be *kāma kusala* for those who will be born in *kāmasugati*, but for those who will be born in woeful state, it must be akusala.
