### Attainment of *Jhānas*

The meditation object changes into the Resembled sign by means of concentration. The concentration on the Resembled sign removes the mental hindrances. When the concentration on the Resembled sign is strong enough, the practitioner attains the 1st *Jhāna* stage.

**Note:** M.O. = One of the 22 Meditation objects.
R.S. = Resembled Sign.
