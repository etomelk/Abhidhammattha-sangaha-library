**In the way of *Abhidhamma***

(1) The same as *Suttanta*,
(2) *Bhavarāga saṁyojana* - Craving for the life of Brahma that is in a state of fetter,
(3) *Paṭigha saṁyojana* - The same as *Suttanta*,
(4) *Māna saṁyojana* - The same as *Suttanta*,
(5) *Diṭṭhi saṁyojana* - The same as *Suttanta*,
(6) *Sīlabbataparāmāsa*- The same as *Suttanta*,
(7) *Vīcikicchā saṁyojana* - The same as *Suttanta*,
(8) *Issā saṁyojana* - Jealousy that is in a state of fetter,
(9) *Macchariya saṁyojana* - Stinginess that is in a state of fetter,
(10) *Avijjā saṁyojana* - The same as *Suttanta*.

Herein, the 9 *Akusala Cetasikas* are mentioned as *Saṁyojana* (fetter). They are *Lobha*, *Dosa*, *Māna*, *Diṭṭhi*, *Vīcikicchā*, *Uddhacca*, *Moha*, *Issā*, *Macchariya*. Among them, *Uddhacca* is said as *Saṁyojana* only in ‘*Suttanta*’ while *Issā* and *Macchariya* are only in ‘*Abhidhamma*’.

Then, *kāmarāga*, *Rūparāga*, *Arūparāga*, *Bhavarāga* refer to only *Lobha*. Therein, *Bhavarāga* in ‘*Abhidhamma*’ is divided into two in ‘*Suttanta*’: *Rūparāga* and *Arūparāga*.
