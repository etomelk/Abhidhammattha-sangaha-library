### *Sammappadhāna*

Right effort is described as 4, referring to its 4 functions.

(1) The effort to remove evil states that have arisen,
(2) The effort to prevent the arising of unarisen evil states,
(3) The effort to develop unarisen wholesome states,
(4) The effort to augment arisen wholesome states.

### *Iddhipāda*

‘The Means of Accomplishment’ is divided into 4:

(1) *Chandiddhipāda* - wish-to-do that is the means of accomplishment,
(2) *Vīriyiddhipāda* - effort that is the means of accomplishment,
(3) *Cittiddhipāda* - *citta* that is the means of accomplishment,
(4) *Vīmaṁsiddhipāda* - investigation that is the means.

### *Indriya*

‘The dominating faculty’ is divided into 5:

(1) *Saddhindriya* - faith that dominates associated states in believing,
(2) *Satindriya* - mindfulness that dominates associated states in minding,
(3) *Vīriyindriya* - effort that dominates associated states in energy,
(4) *Samādhindriya* - concentration that dominates associated states in concentrating,
(5) *Paññindriya* - knowledge that dominates associated states in realizing.
