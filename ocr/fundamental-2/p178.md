**(4) Matter to matter**

There is only one condition, *Indriya (rūpa-jīvitindriya)*, through which matter relates to another matter.

**(5) Matter to mind**

There is only one condition, *Purejāta,* through which matter relates to mind.

**(6) Mind and matter to mind and matter**

There are 9 conditions through which mind and matter relates to mind and matter.

1. *Adhipati*
2. *Sahajāta*
3. *Aññamañña*
4. *Nissaya*
5. *Āhāra*
6. *Indriya*
7. *Vipayutta*
8. *Atthi*
9. *Avigata*

**(7) Mind and matter, *Nibbāna* and *Paññatti* to mind**

There are 2 conditions through which mind, matter, *Nibbāna* and *Paññatti* relate to mind.

1. *Ārammaṇa*
2. *Upanissaya*
