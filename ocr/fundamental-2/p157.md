**To *asaññasatta* plane:**

(1) **Death:** B B M J¹ J² J³ J⁴ J⁵ B C
**Rebirth:** P (9-material group led by *jīvita* occurs as rebirth and run for the whole life together with the possible *kamma*-born matters).

(2) **Death:** B B M J¹ J² J³ J⁴ J⁵ C,
**Rebirth:** P (9-material group led by *jīvita* occurs as rebirth and run for the whole life together with the possible *kamma*-born matters).

**Special Notes:**

In death process in mind-door, the *bhavaṅga* and *cuti* are only one of the 4 *mahāvipāka* with 3-hetu. The *javana*s preceding death are only one of 8 *mahākusala*. They run with the object of *kammanimitta*. In rebirth process the *paṭisandhi* is one of 5 *rūpa jhāna paṭisandhi*s for 15 *rūpa brahmā*s, and for *asaññasatta,* the rebirth starts with the material group of 9 led by *jīvita*.
