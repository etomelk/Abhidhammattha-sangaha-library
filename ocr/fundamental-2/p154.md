There are some beings who will be born from 11 *kāma* planes to 11 *kāma* planes. For them, the 20 types of death and rebirth-process in 5 doors and the 4 in mind-door may possibly arise.

Herein, dealing with eye-door, the death and rebirth-process occurs in 4 ways:

The death consciousness occurs

(1) after *bhavaṅga* preceded by *tadārammaṇa*,

(2) after *bhavaṅga* preceded by *javana*,
