**Dosa-mula Javana and its conditions**

```
                        Arammana
                        ↑
(V)  →  [Dosa]  →  (T)
                        ↓
                        Hadaya Vatthu

Centre:       Dosa
Inner ring:   Phassa, Vedana, Sanna, Cetana, Ekagta, Jvtdrya, Mnskra
Middle ring:  Vitaka, Vicara, Adhimokkha, Viriya, Chanda (the cell after Viriya blank)
Outer ring:   Moha, Ahirika, Anottappa, Uddhacca, Thina, Middha, Kukkca, Maccrya, Issa, Dosa (the other cells blank)
```

```
                        Arammana
                        ↑

                        ↓
                        Hadaya Vatthu

Centre:       Moha

Outer ring:   Moha, Ahirika, Anottappa, Uddhacca, Vicikcha (the other cells blank)
```
