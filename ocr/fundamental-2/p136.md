### 31 Planes and 12 Individuals

There are 31 planes. They are locations of 12 types of individuals. The 12 individuals are classified according to the 31 planes.

**(A) 4 *Puthujjana*s (majority)**

(1) *Duggati Ahetuka* (whose *paṭisandhi* is without *hetu* and in woeful state)

(2) *Sugati Ahetuka* (whose *paṭisandhi* is without hetu, but in blissful state)

(3) *Dvihetuka* (whose paṭisandhi is with 2 hetus)

(4) *Tihetuka* (whose paṭisandhi is with 3 hetus)

**(B) 8 *Ariya*s (Noble)**

(5) *Sotāpattimaggaṭṭha* (with attainment of the 1st path)

(6) *Sotāpattiphalaṭṭha* (with attainment of the 1st fruition)

(7) *Sakadāgāmimaggaṭṭha* (with attainment of the 2nd path)

(8) *Sakadāgāmiphalaṭṭha* (with attainment of the 2nd fruition)

(9) *Anāgāmimaggaṭṭha* (with attainment of the 3rd path)

(10) *Anāgāmiphalaṭṭha* (with attainment of the 3rd fruition)

(11) *Arahattamaggaṭṭha* (with attainment of the 4th path)

(12) *Arahattaphalaṭṭha* (with attainment of the 4th fruition)
