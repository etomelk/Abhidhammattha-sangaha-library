**8 Conditions in *Ārammaṇa* group**

There are 8 conditions that participate in the *ārammaṇa* group.

1. *Ārammaṇa*
2. *Adhipati (Ārammaṇa)*
3. *Nissaya (Purejāta)*
4. *Upanissaya (Ārammaṇa)*
5. *Purejāta (Ārammaṇa)*
6. *Vippayutta (Purejāta)*
7. *Atthi (Purejāta)*
8. *Avigata (Purejāta)*

**15 Conditions in *Sahajāta* group**

There are 15 conditions that participate in the *sahajāta* group. The 15 are divided into 3 - big, medium and small.

The big conditions are 4. They cover all conditions in the 15 *sahajāta* groups:

1. *Sahajāta*
2. *Nissaya*
3. *Atthi*
4. *Avigata*

The medium are also 4. They cover some of the conditions in the *sahajāta* groups:

5. *Aññamañña*
6. *Vipāka*
7. *Sampayutta*
8. *Vipayutta*

But the small are 7. They are particular:

9. *Hetu*
10. *Adhipati*
11. *Āhāra*
