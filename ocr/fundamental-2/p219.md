Then, *Lobha* belongs to 9 categories;
*Diṭṭhi* belongs to 8, except *Nīvaraṇa*;
*Moha* to 7, except *Gantha* and *Upādāna*;
*Dosa* to 5, as *Gantha*, *Nīvaraṇa*, *Anusaya*, *Saṁyojana* and *Kileasa*;
*Vīcikicchā* to 4, as *Nīvaraṇa*, *Anusaya*, *Saṁyojana* and *Kilesa*;
*Māna* to 3, as *Anusaya*, *Saṁyojana* and *Kilesa*;
*Uddhacca* to 3, as *Nīvaraṇa*, *Saṁyojana* and *Kilesa*;
*Thina* to 2, as *Nīvaraṇa* and *Kilesa*;
*Middha* and *Kukkucca* to 1, as *Nīvaraṇa*;
*Ahirika* and *Anottappa* belong to 1, as *Saṁyojana*.
