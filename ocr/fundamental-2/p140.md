### *Arūpa Paṭisandhi*

There are 4 *arūpa paṭisandhi*. They are classified according to their planes respectively.

The 1st *arūpa vipāka* is the *paṭisandhi* of those who are born in the *ākāsānañcāyatana* plane.

The 2nd *arūpa vipāka* is the *paṭisandhi* of those who are born in the *viññāṇañcāyatana* plane.

The 3rd *arūpa vipāka* is the *paṭisandhi* of those who are born in the *ākāsānañcāyatana* plane.

The 4th *arūpa vipāka* is the *paṭisandhi* of those who are born in the *nevasaññānāsaññāyatana* plane.
