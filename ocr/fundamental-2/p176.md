**3 Conditions in *Āhāra* group**

There are 3 conditions that participate in the *āhāra* group.

1. *Āhāra (Rūpa)*
2. *Atthi*
3. *Avigata*

**3 Conditions in *Indriya* group**

There are 3 conditions that participate in the *indriya* group.

1. *Indriya (Rūpajīvita)*
2. *Atthi*
3. *Avigata*

**2 Conditions in *Pakatūpanissaya* group**

There are 2 conditions that participate in the *pakatūpanissaya* group.

1. *Upanissaya*
2. *Kamma*

**1 Condition in the particular *Kamma*:**

1. *Kamma (Nānākkhaṇika)*
