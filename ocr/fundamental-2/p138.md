### (2) *Paṭisandhi* Section

**20 Rebirth states:** Beings are born in one of 31 planes. Their life starts with one of the rebirth states. There are 20 states. They are as follows:

| | |
|---|:-:|
| *Rūpa paṭisandhi* | 1 |
| *Arūpa paṭisandhi* | 19 |
| Total | 20 |

**Rūpa paṭisandhi:** The 9 material groups led by *jīvita* (*jīvita navaka*) are called *rūpa-paṭisandhi*, a material rebirth state. With this state, *asaññasatta* beings in the *brahmā* world start their life. *Asaññasatta* are beings whose rebirth state is just matter.

**Arūpa paṭisandhi:** *Arūpa paṭisandhi* is one of the 19 types of consciousness. The 19 types of consciousness are as follows:

| | |
|---|:-:|
| 1. *Santīraṇa* with neutral feeling | 2 |
| 2. *Mahā vipāka* | 8 |
| 3. *Rūpa vipāka* | 5 |
| 4. *Arūpa vipāka* | 4 |
| Total | 19 |

In the 30 planes excluding *asaññasatta,* all beings start their lives with one of 19 types of rebirth consciousness.

Here are 20 types of *paṭisandhi* states that are classified according to the 31 planes.
