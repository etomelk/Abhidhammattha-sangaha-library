Then, he must attain the 4th *Āruppa*. Immediately after the 2 moments of the 4th *Āruppa*, he is in the state of mental cessation. During the attainment of mental cessation, all minds and mind-born matters come into cessation.

According to limitations made previously, one withdraws from such a state. At that moment, the fruition of Non-returner within an *Anāgāmi*, and the Fruition of Arahantship within an *Arahant* arise only one time.

These 2 attainments are the advantages of *Vipassanā* meditation.
