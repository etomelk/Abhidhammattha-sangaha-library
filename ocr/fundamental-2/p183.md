#### 10. *Purejāta paccayo*

**(A) *Purejāta (Ārammaṇa)***

**Matter to mind**

***Paccaya*:** 18 *nipphanna* matters as object.

***Paccayuppanna*:** 54 *kāma citta*s, 2 *abhiññā*s, and 50 *cetasika*s (excluding 2 *appamañña*s).

**(B) *Purejāta (vatthu)***

**Matter to mind**

***Paccaya*:** 6 base matters.

***Paccayuppanna*:** 85 *citta*s (except 4 *arūpa vipāka*s) and 52 *cetasika*s.

#### 11. *Pacchājāta paccayo*

**Mind to matter**

***Paccaya*:** The following 85 *citta*s (excluding 4 *arūpa vipāka*s) and 52 *cetasika*s.

***Paccayuppanna*:** matters that arise together with preceding minds.

#### 12. *Āsevana paccayo*

**Mind to mind**

***Paccaya*:** 47 preceding mundane *javana citta*s and 52 *cetasika*s.

***Paccayuppanna*:** 51 following *Javana citta*s (excluding 4 *phala citta*s) and 52 *cetasika*s.
