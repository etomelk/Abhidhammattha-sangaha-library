(4) For a person who has **Faithful temperament**, these meditation objects are more suitable: Reflection on the *Buddha*, *Dhamma*, *Saṅgha*, *Sīla*, *Cāga* and Deities.

(5) For a person who has **Intellectual temperament**, these 4 meditation subjects are more suitable: Recollection on Death, Recollection on Peace, the perception of Repulsiveness in food, and Analysis of 4 Elements.

(6) The remaining 14 meditation subjects, namely 10 *Kasiṇa*s and 4 *Āruppa*s, are suitable for all.

It should be noted that in making the Kasiṇa device, a wide one is more suitable for a person with ignorant temperament and a small one for a thoughtful person.

### The 3 Stages of Mental Culture

There are 3 stages of mental culture. They are:

1. *Parikamma bhāvanā* - the Preliminary,
2. *Upacāra bhāvanā* - the Access, and
3. *Appanā bhāvanā* - the Absorption.

All the meditation objects deal with the Preliminary stage of mental culture. But regarding these 10 meditation objects: the 8 Recollections beginning with the recollection on the Buddha, etc., one Perception, and one Analysis; the 2 stages of mental culture are attained: the Preliminary, and the Access. In the 30 remaining meditation objects, all the 3 stages of mental culture are attained.
