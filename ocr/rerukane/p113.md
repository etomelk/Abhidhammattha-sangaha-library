Not bearing up the arrival of *Samaṇa* who destroy the faith of lay people and dishearten them about the Buddha Sāsana (Dispensation of the Buddha) and misguide the lay is not *Kulamacchariya*. Dislike about the arrival of virtuous Bhikkhus is *Kulamacchariya*.

The nature of not bearing up the receipt of requisites such as robes by other Bhikkhus is *Lābhamacchariya*. Some Bhikkhus destroy the requisites offered by the lay due to their misuse. Some Bhikkhus let the requisites offered to them by those expecting merit, to be destroyed by putrefaction and decay without utilising themselves or giving others. When such Bhikkhus receive excessive requisites, to think that it is better if some other good Bhikkhu received them is not *Lābhamacchariya*.

*Vaṇṇa* (beauty) is two-fold as *Vaṇṇa* of the body and *Vaṇṇa* of virtues. *Macchariya* arises about both these. The dislike about the other’s body being as beautiful as yours is *Vaṇṇamacchariya* about the body. Dislike about others being as virtuous as yourself is *Vaṇṇamacchariya* about virtue. The person with *Vaṇṇamacchariya* about the body does not like to talk about or hear about the beauty of others’ bodies. The person with *Vaṇṇamacchariya* about the virtues does not like to talk about or hear about others’ virtue.

Dhamma is two-fold as *Paṭivedha* (realising/understanding) Dhamma and *Pariyatti* (learning) Dhamma. *Paṭivedha* Dhamma is paths fruits and Nibbāna.
