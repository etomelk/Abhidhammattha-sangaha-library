Although desire occurs in *Citta* with greed, in accordance with greed; when improved, it becomes a dhamma which is more powerful and able to control and remove greed. Developed desire becomes masterly and roads to power (*Iddhipāda*). If desire is always in accordance with craving, beings will not be able to shed objects which feed craving and develop great dhamma such as absorptions. Will not be able to attain Nibbāna by controlling and removing craving for sense pleasures and craving for existence. Beings are able to attain them, because, desire can develop to an extent which is capable of removing greed.

### Fourteen unwholesome mental factors

There are fourteen unwholesome mental factors as ***Moha*** **(delusion),** ***Ahirika*** **(lack of moral shame),** ***Anottappa*** **(lack of moral dread),** ***Uddhacca*** **(restlessness),** ***Lobha*** **(greed),** ***Diṭṭhi*** **([wrong] view),** ***Māna*** **(conceit),** ***Dosa*** **(hatred),** ***Issā*** **(jealousy),** ***Macchariya*** **(avarice/stinginess),** ***Kukkucca*** **(worry/remorse),** ***Thīna*** **(sloth),** ***Middha*** **(torpor) and** ***Vicikiccā*** **(skeptical doubt).**
