“***Ekaṃ mahābhūtaṃ tinnaṃ mahābhūtānaṃ nissaya paccayena paccayo, tayo mahā bhūtā ekassa mahābhūtassa nissaya paccayena paccayo, dve mahā bhūtā dvinnaṃ mahābhūtānaṃ nissaya paccayena paccayo***”

Although great elements are not materials, extremely small materials called the corporeal groups arise due to the arising of the great elements embracing and attaching to each other. It is an extremely small material. The atom which is considered to be extremely small is made up by the combination of a large number of corporeal groups. It is the four great elements which are found in any material such as soil, water, fire, wind, gold, silver, pearls, gems, bones, blood and flesh. They are formed by the combination of great elements. These materials which are formed by the combination of the four great elements are of various types due to the differences in the composition. The four elements are not found to the same extent in all objects. The earth element is more in certain objects. The water element is more in another. The fire element is more in certain objects. The wind element is more in another. One element is prevalent in an object while the other three are weak. Two elements are pronounced and two are weak in some.
