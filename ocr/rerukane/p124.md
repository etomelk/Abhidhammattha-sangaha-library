#### *Sati*

Awareness or the nature of remembering the wholesome side is *Sati*. The minds of people with defilements are always bent on the unwholesome side. Spreads on the unwholesome side. It travels in the unwholesome side as the pumpkin shell which floats down the flowing river. Mindfulness prevents this and keeps the mind on the wholesome side. It is due to this mindfulness that beings perform wholesome acts and wholesome consciousness arises in them.
