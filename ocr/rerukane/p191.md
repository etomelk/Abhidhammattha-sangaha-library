The twelve corporealities comprising the five sensitive corporealities (*Pasāda rūpa*) and the seven physically contactable corporealities (*Vishaya rūpa*) are corporealities which impact with each other. Therefore they become **gross corporealities (*Oudārika rūpa*).** The water element, two gender corporealities, heart as physical base, life corporeality, food corporeality, corporeality of separation (*Paricceda rūpa*), two corporealities of expression, three changeable corporealities (*Vikāra rūpa*) and the four characteristic corporealities do not impact on others. They are **subtle corporealities (*Sukshama rūpa*)**. The eight corporealities of colour, odour, taste, nutriment and the four elements are called ***Avinibbhoga rūpa*** **(Inseparable corporealities),** because they are never separated from each other.
