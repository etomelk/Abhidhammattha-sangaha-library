#### *Uddhacca*

When a wet lump of clay is dropped on the floor, it remains quietly attached to the floor. The rubber ball dropped on the floor does not remain still but keeps popping up. Even among *Citta*, some remain quietly in the object, like the lump of clay dropped on the floor. Some *Citta* keeps moving in the object like the rubber ball. It is the *Ekaggatā Cetasika* called concentration which creates this nature of not moving about in the object. The motion in the restless *Citta* is caused by *Uddhacca*. The nature of moving or jumping up of the *Citta* is **Uddhacca.** Stated in another manner, the nature of movement should be called *Uddhacca*. Non-material *dhamma* do not move
