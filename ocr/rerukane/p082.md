#### *Cetanā*

It is difficult to write and explain to others, the nature of the mental factor of *Cetanā* ([mental] volition). I do not know whether it is due to a weakness in our [Sinhala] language, our own weakness or the deepness of Dhamma. This description is made according to our ability as at present. Mind is, knowing objects. It does not have any strength to perform any other action. The body, from which the mind has departed, remains like a piece of firewood. Therefore, it should be known that, the body too has no strength or intention to perform actions. Although the nature of both mind and the body are such, it can be seen that thousands of actions such as going, coming, sitting, eating and drinking take place. They occur due a particular type of power which arises along with the mind. *Cetanā* is the kind of power which causes the performance of physical, verbal and mental actions.

When a man strikes a dog, the mind does only the taking in of the object called dog. When this *Citta* occurs along with the volition which is the attempt and energy to strike, the striking will take place by some part of the body being moved towards the dog’s body due to the power of a wind created by it.
