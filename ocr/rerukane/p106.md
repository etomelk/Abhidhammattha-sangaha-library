#### *Diṭṭhi*

Although the word *Diṭṭhi* is applicable to both right view and wrong view, it refers to wrong view, in this description of unwholesome mental factors. Just as matters are conceived correctly according to various reasons and proprieties; the nature of seeing the matter incorrectly according to wrong reasons is *Diṭṭhi* ([wrong] view). Those with *Diṭṭhi* see it as right view. Although *Diṭṭhi* is one as seeing incorrectly, there are many variations of it. *Sāssata Diṭṭhi* (eternity belief) and *Uccheda Diṭṭhi* (annihilation view) are the two main *Diṭṭhi*. Many other *Diṭṭhi* spread from these two. Many *Diṭṭhi* arise from *Sāssata Diṭṭhi*. *Natthika Diṭṭhi* (nihilism), *Ahetuka Diṭṭhi* (View of uncausedness) and *Akiriya Diṭṭhi* (view of inefficacy of action) are three *Diṭṭhi* which become unwholesome courses of action (*Kammapatha*). There are ten variations of *Natthika Diṭṭhi* such as the view that giving alms is of no use. They are called *Dasavatthuka Miccā Diṭṭhi* (ten-fold wrong view). There are twenty *Sakkāya Diṭṭhi* (personality belief). There are sixty two *Diṭṭhi* shown in the Brahmajāla Sutta.
