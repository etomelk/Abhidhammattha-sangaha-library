*Bhavāṅga*. The meaning of the term *Bhavāṅga Citta*[^26] is that it is the mind that assists in the existence of life. Incidental generations of *Citta* which takes various objects such as form, and performs various functions are those which arise from the *Bhavāṅga* generation of *Citta*. *Bhavāṅga* generation of *Citta* exists at all times when *Citta* pertaining to other matters do not arise. This *Bhavāṅga Citta* is also referred to as the root *Citta* or natural *Citta*. It is also called mind door (*Manodvāra*) as it causes various processes of consciousness (*Citta-vīthi*).

It was about the *Bhavāṅga Citta* that the Buddha stated, “***Pahassaramidaṃ bhikkhave cittaṃ***”. Its beauty is its purity. That purity is like an *opaya*[^27]. Taking in images of things close by, into itself is the nature of *opaya*. *Bhavāṅga Citta* has such a nature. Images of all objects such as form and sound impact the *Bhavāṅga Citta*. Even if these objects impact on the eye and the like, consciousness such as seeing, hearing will not arise, if they do not impact the *Bhavāṅga Citta* consisting the mind door. Consciousness arises by the impact of objects on the *Bhavāṅga Citta*. The impact of objects on the *Bhavāṅga Citta* also occurs through the five doors.
