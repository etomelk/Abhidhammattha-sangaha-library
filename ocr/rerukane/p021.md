Whether *Abhidhamma Piṭaka* is a teaching of the Buddha or not, the only means available for the understanding of Dhamma is studying Abhidhamma. There are seven *Pakaraṇa* as, **Dhammasaṅgaṇī Pakaraṇa, Vibhaṅga Pakaraṇa, Dhātukathā Pakaraṇa, Puggala Paññatti Pakaraṇa, Kathāvattu Pakaraṇa, Yamaka Pakaraṇa** and **Paṭṭhāna Pakaraṇa** in the Abhidhamma Piṭaka. Among these seven *Pakaraṇa*; **Dhammasaṅgaṇī Pakaraṇa, Dhātukathā Pakaraṇa** and **Yamaka Pakaraṇa** should be committed to memory. In Myanmar (Burma), these books are taught in the dark at night. Teaching of *Abhidhamma* is divided into two as *divāvācanaya* (Reading from books during the daytime) and *rātrīvācanaya* (Recitation with someone at night). The books mentioned above as those to be committed to memory are *rātrīvācanaya*. The rest are *divāvācanaya*.

*Abhidhamma rātrīvācanaya* is not something to be memorized like Bālāvatāra, Sidatsaṅgarā and the like which are committed to memory by young ones. Even the entire life time will not be sufficient to do so. The student, who has methodically studied books such as Abhidhamma Saṅgrahaya and got matters explained by teachers, can arrange and make the *rātrīvācanaya*. Learning *Abhidhamma* is not reading the books, but comprehending the *Pakaraṇa* in Pali as well as in own language and understanding it.
