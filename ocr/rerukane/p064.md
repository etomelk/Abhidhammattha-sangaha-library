Similarly, as fine and deep matters do not impact on *Bhavāṅga Citta* lacking in clarity and are weak and dark; *Citta* focusing on fine and deep matters do not arise in the mind base of those who have unclean *Bhavāṅga Citta*. Therefore, such people do not understand difficult matters. Even if they hear what others say, they do not comprehend it. Even if they read books, deep matters in them are not comprehended. It is the people with *Bhavāṅga Citta,* lacking in purity that are considered as fools and those who cannot think.

Because the *Bhavāṅga* arises due to a past *kamma*, one being will only have one type of *Bhavāṅga Citta* during one life. The person who has a dark, weak *Bhavāṅga Citta* from birth, in whom *Citta* capable of knowing deep matters do not arise cannot be made into a person with a bright *Bhavāṅga* mind base, by whatever means. That is why stupidity inherited at birth remains even in old age. That is why the Buddha stated the following:

> *“Yāvajīvampi ce bālo - paṇḍitaṃ payarupāsati*
>
> *na so dhammaṃ vijānāti - dabbī sūparasaṃ yathā”*

“The ignorant will not understand Dhamma even if he associates the learned over a long period, just as the spoon used for stirring will only be worn out and will not feel the taste of the curry.”
