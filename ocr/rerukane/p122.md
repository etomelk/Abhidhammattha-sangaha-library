The doubt about the past-future is, doubting about the aggregates, bases and elements belonging to the past and the future.

The eighth skeptical doubt shown as “*Idapaccayatā paṭicca samuppannesu dhammesu kaṅkhati*” is the doubt about the dependent origination (*Paṭicca Samuppāda*). Skeptical doubt has been shown in six ways in the **Sabbāsava Sutta**.

Both restlessness and skeptical doubt are mental factors which waver in the object. Restlessness wavers in the object without expectation of anything else. Skeptical doubt wavers about the object as, is it like this or is it like that and is it this or is it that. This is the difference between these two mental factors.
