### Life corporeality

The corporeality that controls and maintains the generation of *kamma*-produced corporeality is life corporeality (*Jīvita rūpa*). Life vitality (*jīvitindriya*) is another name for it. There are four types of corporeality caused by the four reasons of *kamma*, *citta*, *utu* (climate) and *āhāra* (food) found to be in individual bodies. Among them, the causes of corporealities resulting from the three reasons such as *citta* [excluding *kamma*], remain in close proximity to the corporealities and are bound to them. The causes such as *citta* are capable of producing corporealities only during their life time. Therefore, the corporealities which are caused by reasons such as *citta* last while the reasons are present and go into extinction along with the causes. They do not have the strength to survive after the death of the causes. Therefore, they are corporealities without life.
