### Two gender corporealities

There are two gender corporealities as **corporeality of femininity and corporeality of masculinity**. The bodies of all beings in the world are constructed with the four great elements. There should be a special reason for the arising of two types of bodies as feminine and masculine, both of which are made up of the same elements. That special reason is the gender corporeality. It occurs due to *kamma*. Not only in the bodies of men and women, there is the distinction of male-female nature even in craving. The craving that arises about matters pertaining only to women is female craving. The craving that arises about matters pertaining only to men is male craving. Craving is also a powerful reason for the repeated birth of beings in various lives. Feminine corporeality arises along with re-birth, due to a *kamma* with unovercome female craving.
