People who believe in an 'eternal heaven' may carry their belief to the deva plane and take the long life span there to be an eternal existence. Only those who have known the Dhamma will realise that, as these planes are impermanent, some day these sentient beings will fall away from them and be reborn elsewhere. The devas can help people by inclining their minds to wholesome acts, and people can help the devas by inviting them to rejoice in their meritorious deeds.

*The fine material sphere* (*rūpa loka*) consists of sixteen planes. Beings take rebirth into these planes as a result of attaining the jhānas. They have bodies made of fine matter. The sixteen planes correspond to the attainment of the four jhānas as follows:

A. Three as a result of attaining the First Jhāna:

a) *brahma parisajjā* - realm of Brahma's retinue (12)

b) *brahma purohitā* - realm of Brahma's ministers (13)

c) *mahā brahmā* - realm of great Brahmā (14).

B. Three as a result of attaining the Second Jhāna:

a) *parittābhā* - realm of minor lustre (15)

b) *appamānābhā* - realm of infinite lustre (16)

c) *ābhassarā* - realm of radiant lustre (17).

C. Three as a result of attaining the Third Jhāna:

a) *paritta subhā* - realm of minor aura (18)

b) *appamānasubhā* - realm of infinite aura (19)

c) *subha kiṇhā* - realm of steady aura (20)
