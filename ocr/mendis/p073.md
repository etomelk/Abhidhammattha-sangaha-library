c) *Decisive-support condition*: This acts by virtue of its cogency. It is of three kinds:

1. By way of an object (*ārammaṇa upanissaya paccaya*). The image of the Buddha, at the time of veneration, forms an object decisive support condition for the establishment of faith by way of conviction.

2. Proximate decisive support (*anantara upanissaya paccaya*). When one thought gives way to the next, the conviction in one stands as a decisive support for the thought that follows.

3. Natural decisive support (*pakati upanissaya paccaya*). Faith, virtue, generosity and learning, by way of cogency, stand as natural decisive supports for the repeated arising of these wholesome factors. A good environment and companionship with the wise are natural decisive supports for wholesome mental states.

These three types of decisive support conditions have a bearing on our practice if we wish to fulfill the four preliminary conditions to stream entry (*sotāpattiyanga*). These are: (i) companionship with those of merit and good character (*sappurisa saṁseva*); (ii) hearing the Dhamma (*saddhamma savana*); (iii) wise reflection (*yoniso manasikāra*); and (iv) living in conformity with the Dhamma (*dhammānudhammapatipatti*).
