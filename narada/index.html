Chapters I–VII and IX, one per page, after the Preface. Nārada numbers his sections § 1, § 2 … in each chapter; a chip beside a section opens the same Pāli in the Sixth Council Saṅgaha and the Vibhāvinī. Chapter VIII (Paccaya) is missing from this copy: its contents list it, but the text runs from Chapter VII to Chapter IX.
