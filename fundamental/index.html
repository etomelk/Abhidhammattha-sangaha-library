Fundamental Abhidhamma, Part I

Dr. Ashin Nandamālābhivaṃsa, Sagaing Hills · Centre for Buddhist Studies (CBS), Mahāsubodhayon Monastery, Sagaing, Myanmar

Part I, one chapter per page: the Editorial Preface, About the Author and The History of Abhidhamma, then the five chapters of Part I. Its chapters are in its own order — 1 Citta, 2 Cetasika, 3 Rūpa (the Saṅgaha’s chapter VI), 4 Pakiṇṇaka (III), 5 Vīthi (IV) — and the Saṅgaha map links each to the Saṅgaha’s chapter. Part II (from Vīthi on) is in a second copy whose text layer is unreadable; it waits for OCR.
