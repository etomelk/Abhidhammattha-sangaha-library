Fundamental Abhidhamma, Parts I–II

Dr. Ashin Nandamālābhivaṃsa, Sagaing Hills · Centre for Buddhist Studies (CBS), Mahāsubodhayon Monastery, Sagaing, Myanmar

One chapter per page. Part I: the Editorial Preface, About the Author and The History of Abhidhamma, then its five chapters — 1 Citta, 2 Cetasika, 3 Rūpa (the Saṅgaha’s chapter VI), 4 Pakiṇṇaka (III), 5 Vīthi (IV). Part II begins again with Chapter 5 Vīthi in a new, longer wording, then 6 Vīthimutta (V), 7 Paccaya (VIII), 8 Kammaṭṭhāna (IX), 9 Samuccaya (VII), and the colour charts that close the book. The Saṅgaha map links each chapter to the Saṅgaha’s chapter. Part II was proofread from a scan; the book’s own misprints are kept.
