                  Process of Consciousness and Matter

                  (Aggamahāpaṇḍita) Bhaddanta Dr. Rewata Dhamma · edited by (Aggamahāpaṇḍita) Ven. Dr. Kumarabhivamsa · Triple Gem Publications, 2004

                  The whole book: the Preface, Acknowledgement and biography, the Introduction, its five chapters, the eight tables of kalāpas, the Notes, Bibliography, Pāli-English Glossary and Index. The book’s page numbers are kept in the margin (and can be reached as #pg113 or #p113, so a chart’s “PCM p113” can be found; the Go to box takes a page number); the index links to them. The notes are endnotes numbered by chapter: each reference opens its note in the Notes. The Saṅgaha map links Chapter I to the Saṅgaha’s chapter I, Chapters II–IV to chapter IV and Chapter V to chapter VI. The book’s own misprints are kept as printed.
