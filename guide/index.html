Guide Through the Abhidhamma-Piṭaka

Nyanatiloka Mahāthera · edited by Nyanaponika Thera · Buddhist Publication Society, Kandy

The whole book, one part per page: the prefaces and introduction, the Abhidhamma Mātikā in translation, the seven books — Dhammasaṅgaṇī, Vibhaṅga, Dhātukathā, Puggalapaññatti, Kathāvatthu, Yamaka, Paṭṭhāna — and the appendix on Dependent Origination.
