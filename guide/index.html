Guide Through the Abhidhamma-Piṭaka

Nyanatiloka Mahāthera · edited by Nyanaponika Thera · Buddhist Publication Society, Kandy

The whole book, one part per page: the publisher’s preface and the introduction, the Abhidhamma Mātikā in translation, the seven books — Dhammasaṅgaṇī, Vibhaṅga, Dhātukathā, Puggalapaññatti, Kathāvatthu, Yamaka, Paṭṭhāna — Appendix I on Dependent Origination and Appendix II, the tables.
